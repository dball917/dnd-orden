# Surviving the First Day

## Session Info

* **Date**: 2026-03-24
* **Characters**: [[Barabaras]], [[Grafgar]], [[Glaucon]], [[Holly]]
* **Next Session**: Tuesday, April 21st

## Summary

We spread out to talk to the other survivors to figure out how to help. Glaucon diffuses tension between the first-classers (Vivek, Mr. Burtonfrank, & Kara) and the marines before eventually preparing a meal for everybody (with help from Marta, Gran, & Grafgar). The orcs (Irkin, Carmac, & Serena) collect tools to start the process of taking down trees to build shelter.

With everything moving in the right direction (mostly), we leave to track down the marine (Virgil) whom chased after a giant rat that stole a large bag of grain. After a struggle, we kill the rats and find Virgil's corpse. Since we take a while to process the rat meat, we get attacked by a swarm of stirges.
