## Summary

We return to camp with the rat meat and Virgil's corpse. We see that the others have made progress with lean-to structures and a large pile of wood.

After attempting to come up with a meat preservation plan, we work with the others to make a pyre for Virgil. Lieutenant Jules eulogizes him before we all rest for the night.

In the morning, we check on our goblin friends and offer them rat meat. They already trapped some of their own. We learn how they've been doing, then we head off to explore the barricaded cave at the rat's nest.

We get through the rotten wood barricade before venturing into the dark cave. We find a large room with smooth stone walls and ceiling interspersed with rough, natural parts. We find several corpses lying around, a large tent with a desk, and an inactive magical lift.

Barabaras climbs the lift chain up 3 floors to peek ahead. On the 4th floor, he is chased away by a specter. We continue exploring and survive being attacked by several skeletons and zombies.
