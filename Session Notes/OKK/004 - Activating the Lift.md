# Activating the Lift

## Session Info

* **Date**: 2026-50-19
* **Characters**: [[Barabaras]], [[Grafgar]], [[Glaucon]], [[Holly]]
* **Next Session**: Wednesday, June 24th

## Summary

We continue searching the place. We make our way to a room with a telescope, where we fight a specter and some zombies. We activate the lift, find a sunlit room with a window, and find several magical items. We take a short rest to identify the magical items.

Loot:
- silvered dagger
- 3 lesser healing potions
- dark purplish book (scholarly astrology book in an archaic version of Common; notes in the margins in modern Common)
- book cover with a "star" on it
- 500gp worth of precious gems
- Amulet of Protection
- Wind Fan
- Violin of Illusions
- Pole of Collapsing
## Notes

- The charm on the amulet is shaped like a shield with 2 links and a chain.
- We go back downstairs to check on the other corpses. None remain, but Glaucon picks up a silvered dagger in the tent.
- We search the second floor crates and shelves.
	- Glaucon & Holly find that the crates are worn, so we don't know what they say. They previously held supplies.
	- Barabaras searches the bookshelves where there is a sparse assortment of books and dried paper on the floor.
	- Grafgar looks into the lift.
	- Holly gets Grafgar to make a crowbar to pry open a crate. Inside one are 3 lesser healing potions (glass vials with ruby red liquid). Not much else is useful.
	- Barabaras finds an intact dark purplish book and puts it in his bag.
	- Barabaras puts on the amulet. It makes him feel safer. He doesn't see anything with the lift terminal.
	- Glaucon replaces Barabaras searching the bookshelves and finds a book with a "star" on the cover. He takes the cover since the rest of the book fell apart.
	- Barabaras starts making a shoddy rope latter in the lift chain to help us climb up.
	- Grafgar checks out the purplish book (History 23) and recognizes it being in a very archaic version of Common. It has more recent common notes in the margins.
	- Glaucon tries to comprehend the book and we learn that the book is a scholarly book on astrology.
- We climb up the lift chain in order: Grafgar, Barabaras, Holly, then Glaucon.
- The third floor is just the elevator room and the rock wall.
- We climb above the 4th floor without leaving the chain (to avoid the specter). Glaucon tosses a piece of leather to the floor, and nothing happens.
- We get off the chain, and nothing happens. We proceed down the ramp.
- We find a room with a dome ceiling painted like the night sky.
- The capstan on the ceiling has a chain connecting to the capstan above the lift.
- There's a large telescope.
- The room has a dais with a body on it and desks on the edges.
- Just a people were about to question Barabaras, the specter attacks us.
- We take out the specter, then 3 zombies stand up. We take them out then search the room.
- The telescope can zoom/focus but cannot change direction.
- The desks have some astrology notes.
- On a corpse, we find a stone disk with a rune.
- On the pedestal/dais, there are symbols similar to the lift terminal. There looks to be a slot for a disk. Glaucon sticks it in. The runes light up on the pedestal and the lift console.
- Grafgar fiddles with the console. The chain goes up and chews up the rope. A platform comes up and stops at our level. It's covered in algae.
- Glaucon starts cleaning the platform with prestidigitation.
- The notes on the desk are about the observation here.
- We mess with the console enough to figure out how to move it between levels. And we can tell there are several floors underwater.
- We walk down the stairs to the third floor. There's a sunlit camp and a window. (The overwhelming sense of dread is gone.)
- Crates used to have food but are now empty and have holes from the rats. The side shows 2 links in a chain. There are 6-ish bedrolls. There's a mendable tent with a cot, a rotted dressed, and an ornate chest.
- Glaucon opens the chest. It contains precious gems (500 gp worth of gems); a paper fan of exceptional quality in unusually good condition; 2 small cases with a violin (exceptionally fine quality with filagree); and a 1-ft rod with a world engraved on its case.
- We take a short rest to learn about the items:
	- 500 gp worth of precious gems
	- Amulet of Protection - +1 AC or +1 to save; requires attunement
	- Wind Fan - Once per day cast Gust of Wind. Can cast again for a cumulative +20% chance to break. The chance resets at dawn.
	- Violin of Illusions - When played, minor illusion lights appear that are obviously illusions. (5ft range or 15ft if bard)
	- Pole of Collapsing - 1ft pole that extends to 10ft or collapses back with a command word
